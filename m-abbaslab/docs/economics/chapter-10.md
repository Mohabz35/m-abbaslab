# Economic Forecasting Model
## Section 10: Security, Ethics & Limitations

---

### 10.1 Data Security
- **Encryption**: At rest and in transit.
- **Access Control**: Role-based access (RBAC) to sensitive indicators.
- **Audit Logs**: Tracking all training and inference requests.

### 10.2 Ethical Considerations
- **Probabilistic Nature**: Forecasts are estimates, not guarantees.
- **Human-in-the-loop**: System results should complement, not replace, expert judgment.
- **Transparency**: Avoiding "black-box" models by providing interpretable metrics.

---

### 10.3 System Limitations
- **Data Quality Sensitivity**: Dependent on the accuracy of reporting agencies.
- **Unprecedented Shocks**: Models may underperform during "Black Swan" events.
- **Contextual Ignorance**: Quantitative models may miss qualitative geopolitical shifts.

### 10.4 Responsible Use
Users are advised to use these tools for supporting research and strategy rather than automated high-stakes decision-making.
