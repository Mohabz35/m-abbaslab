# Economic Forecasting Model
## Section 9: Interactive Visualization & Analytics

---

### 9.1 Dashboard Objectives
The dashboard serves to bridge the gap between complex models and decision-makers by communicating trends clearly, displaying uncertainty, and providing interactive controls.

### 9.2 Visualization Features
- **Time-Series Charts**: High-performance rendering of historical and predicted data.
- **Forecast Bands**: Visualizing confidence intervals (95% uncertainty).
- **Comparison Views**: Statistical breakdown of historical vs predicted performance.

---

### 9.3 Analytics Capabilities
- **Indicator Selection**: Users can switch between GDP, Inflation, and other series.
- **Horizon Adjustment**: Dynamic shifting of the forecast window.
- **Report Generation**: Exportable summaries and raw data downloads.

### 9.4 Interpretation
Dashboards include tooltips and explanations to help users understand why a specific trend is emerging based on model inputs.
